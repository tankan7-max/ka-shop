One small set-up step is needed. In Supabase, open SQL Editor → New query, paste the contents of supabase-thrift.sql (in the repository) and press Run. Then reload this page. Until then the thrift shop keeps using the items in thrift.json.
The database has no thrift items yet. The shop is using the list in thrift.json. Copy that list in once, and from then on you manage everything here.
How this works Add an item with photos, price and condition. It appears in the shop straight away. What you paid, where you got it and your notes are only for you: shoppers never see them.
3D model: every item stands on the shelf as a model. Pick a ready-made shape (book, mug, vase, box, folded clothes…), set its real size in cm, and adjust turn, tilt, corners, taper, neck, handle, lid, colour and shine until it looks right. Your photos wrap onto it, and you control how: front only, wrapped round, two photos (front and back), or tiled, with stretch, slide, rotate, flip, brightness, colour and tint. Items you have not set up get a suggested model from their category or name. You can also upload your own .glb file.
Reserve an item when someone has asked for it (it shows as Reserved and can't be added to a bag). Sold records the price you got, the date and who bought it, and feeds the profit figures above. Relist brings a sold item back.
Hide (untick “Show in the shop”) keeps an item in your records without showing it. Items that have sat unsold for more than 60 days get a flag, so you know when to drop the price.
Download backup saves the shop's items as thrift.json; the shop uses that file if the database is unreachable. Export CSV saves everything, including your private columns, for a spreadsheet.
