How this works Add a product with its name, price and pack, pick the pack shape that looks most like it, and upload a photo of the front of the pack. The shop builds the 3D pack from that. The editor shows a live 3D preview of the pack as you adjust it. Tick “the photo is the flat artwork” when your picture is just the label or front panel (no background) and it will be wrapped right across the pack.
For anything the built-in shapes cannot do, upload a 3D model (a .glb file). It replaces the built-in shape for that product, is sized to the width, height and depth you enter, and should face forward (+Z) and stand upright (+Y).
Hide a product to take it off the shelves without losing it; Delete removes it for good. A new product starts with the stock you enter; after that, change stock on the Stock page.
Download backup saves everything as products.json. The shop falls back to that file if the database cannot be reached, so upload a fresh copy to the repository now and then.

How the pack looks
Sizes are in shop units, about 3× real life so packs read well on the shelf: a real 15 cm-wide bag is about 0.45. Picking a shape fills in typical sizes.
Photo of the pack front
JPEG, PNG or WebP. It is made smaller before uploading. Without a photo the shop draws the pack from the name and colour.
This photo is only a link to another website, not a file in your shop. The small preview above works for any link, but the 3D pack needs that website’s permission to use its picture, and most websites do not give it. When that happens the pack shows the plain drawn pack instead.
If copying is refused, save the picture to your computer and upload it with “Choose file” above.

Zooms the photo or label on the pack face and slides it around, so you can fit it to cover the face. 100% is the pack as the shop draws it today.
3D model (optional)
A .glb file up to 8 MB, without Draco compression. Keep it low-poly: every pack on the shelf is a copy.
